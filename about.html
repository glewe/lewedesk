Everything a support team needs

Issue tracking

Report issues and request improvements. Every issue has a type, status, product, tags, markdown description, comments and a full history.

Configurable workflow

Define your own issue types, statuses, resolutions and products so the helpdesk follows the way your team works.

Fine-grained access control

Roles, groups and permissions decide who sees what. Issues are public, visible to registered users or private to the reporter.

Notifications

Stay informed with in-app and email notifications, set up per user and per event.

Attachments and search

Attach files to issues and comments, and find any issue quickly with the built-in search and saved views.

Secure by design

Two-factor authentication, optional LDAP or SSO (OIDC), spam protection and an audit log keep your data safe.

Frequently asked questions

What is LeweDesk?

LeweDesk is a web-based customer support helpdesk and ticket management system by Lewe Labs. Customers report issues and request improvements, and support staff track them to resolution.

Who is it for?

Software vendors and service teams that want a structured, central place for customer reports, feature requests and support conversations.

Which languages does it support?

The interface is available in English and German.

Who can see my issues?

Each issue is either public, visible to registered users only, or private to the person who reported it. Internal comments stay hidden from reporters.
